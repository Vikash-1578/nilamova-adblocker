# Nilamova Ad Blocker — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

## Short version
**We collect nothing.** Nilamova Ad Blocker does not collect, store, sell or send any personal data, browsing history or website content to anyone, including us.

**Hum kuch bhi collect nahi karte.** Ye extension aapka data, browsing history ya kisi website ka content kahin nahi bhejta. Sab kuch aapke computer par hi rehta hai.

## What the extension stores on your device
- Your settings (on/off, enabled categories, language)
- Sites you allowed (whitelist) and elements you removed with the element picker
- Simple counters (how many ads/popups were blocked)

This is saved only in your browser's local extension storage and is deleted when you uninstall the extension.

## Why it needs permissions
- **Read and change data on all websites:** to block ad requests and hide ad elements on the pages you visit.
- **Tabs / navigation:** to close ad popups and show a warning before dangerous sites.
- **Storage:** to save your settings on your device.

No remote code is loaded. All filter rules are bundled inside the extension.
